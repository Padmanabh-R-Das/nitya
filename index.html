02 · RECALIBRATION

Questions

The things people actually ask

I've abandoned every planner I've tried. Why is this different?
Because you never have to open it to keep it alive. Every planner you abandoned required you to maintain it — to re-plan after a bad week, which is precisely when you have least appetite for it. Nitya does that step itself — on the founder's instance it runs unattended at 06:11 every morning; on the current shared version it re-plans the moment you open the page, before you decide anything. Either way, you never rebuild a broken week by hand: you only mark what happened.
Does it actually make me do the work?
No, and anyone claiming otherwise is selling you something. It removes the decision load and the dead-plan problem, which is what defeats most people who are already willing to work. If you genuinely do not want to do the work, this will simply tell you so, weekly, in a number.
Who is it for? Can I use it for UPSC, NEET or JEE?
Nitya is built for students in CS, IT, AI / data science and allied branches: DSA, competitive programming, software jobs and internships, ML, CS exams like GATE CS / DA, and their CS college courses. Its resources, contest calendar and ladders are all from that world. It is not built for UPSC, NEET, JEE, CA, banking or other non-CS preparation, and it won't pretend to be.
What do you do with my data?
Your plan and your record are yours and are not sold, shared or used to train anything. Each user's data is private to that user. You can export your full record at any time.
How many people use it?
Two, today. We would rather tell you that than invent a number. One is the founder, running it daily since March 2026 with a complete log; the other is a student using a shared instance. The first cohort is how that changes, and it is why it is free.
Who built this?
One founder — a Data Science & AI undergraduate at IIIT Dharwad — who built it to solve his own problem and has run it on himself every day for eight months. It is not a weekend project or a mock-up.
